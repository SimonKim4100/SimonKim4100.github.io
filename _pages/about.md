I'm Semin (Simon) Kim, a master's student at [Yonsei University](https://yonsei.ac.kr/sc/index.do), advised by Prof. [Jongyoo Kim](https://mmai.yonsei.ac.kr/cv/) at the [Multimedia AI Lab](https://mmai.yonsei.ac.kr/). My research interests are in 3D computer vision, especially human-object interaction, human mesh recovery, and 3D reconstruction. Before graduate school, I received my bachelor's degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Yonsei University.
